## v1.10.2 full-assertion 360-day multi-seed simulations (external market + recipe lifecycle + CP tuning)

With the `computeFast` combat-stat optimization in `js/stats.js` and `STATS.now` initialization in `js/sim.js`, the full-assertion `test/long_simulation.js` harness now completes 360 simulated days (518,400 native ticks) with daily invariant checks on every tick/day:

| Seed | Simulated days | Complete ticks | Final living pop | CP minted | CP burned | Final CP price | Visitor arrivals | Sect bought / sold (units) | Sect stones out / in | Formulas found / auths | Elapsed ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v1102-seed1 | 360 | 518,400 | 64 | 74,365 | 51,694 | 1.12 | 61 | 533 / 1,032 | 8,182 / 12,528 | 8/8 (26 auths) | 315,453 |
| v1102-seed2 | 360 | 518,400 | 54 | 86,748 | 65,528 | 0.98 | 54 | 667 / 821 | 9,571 / 13,367 | 8/8 (28 auths) | 340,503 |

Both seeds **PASS** all daily assertions (`test/results/long_v1102_seed1.json`, `test/results/long_v1102_seed2.json`):
- **CP price equilibrium**: Final CP price index stabilized at **1.12** (`v1102-seed1`) and **0.98** (`v1102-seed2`), avoiding the previous 2.18–4.00 inflation drift while maintaining active purchasing (879 and 952 shop purchases), paid training (23,272 and 21,545 CP burned), master guidance (1,575 and 3,585 CP burned), and recipe authorization fees (271 and 269 CP burned).
- **External market balance**: Across both 360-day seeds, sect surplus exports (12,528 and 13,367 stones received) exceeded external imports (8,182 and 9,571 stones spent), and members actively bought and sold with personal spirit stones (46 buys / 40 sells in `v1102-seed1`; 47 buys / 38 sells in `v1102-seed2`).
- **Recipe scroll lifecycle & natural production**: In `v1102-seed1`, 1 formula was kept personally, traded between members, and all 8 entered `knownRecipes` with 26 crafter authorizations; `SRC110_MW_REC_006` (detox/qi pill), `SRC110_MW_REC_005`, and `SRC110_MW_REC_022` were crafted naturally. In `v1102-seed2`, `SRC110_MW_REC_045` (tier-2 talisman), `SRC110_MW_REC_022`, and `SRC110_MW_REC_044` were crafted naturally, and ingredients for `SRC110_MW_REC_048` (tier-3 array) were reserved and consumed.
